File Converter: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

File Converter is an app by Creature Comforts for converting documents, reading text from scans (OCR), and merging and splitting PDFs. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Your files stay on your device

Every conversion, OCR job, merge and split runs entirely on your device. The files you choose, and everything made from them, are never uploaded to us or to anyone else. Results are saved on your device (on Android, in the Download/File Converter folder), where only you and the apps you choose can open them.

What we collect

Nothing. File Converter has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics of its own. We don't collect, store, sell or share your personal information, your files or their contents.

Google components the app uses

Reading text in scans and photos uses Google ML Kit text recognition, which runs on the device. Scanning paper documents with the camera uses the ML Kit document scanner, provided by Google Play services. Google may receive limited diagnostic and usage information about these components to keep them working, as described in Google's ML Kit data disclosure and the Google Privacy Policy. The text recognised in your documents is not sent to Google.

If you buy the app, the purchase is handled by Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We don't receive your payment details.

Permissions

The app asks for one permission: vibration, for the touch feedback you can turn off in Settings. You pick each file yourself through Android's file picker, or by sharing it to the app, so it never needs access to all your files. The document scanner uses the camera through Google Play services, only while you're scanning.

Children

File Converter isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
